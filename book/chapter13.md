> UNII Corps log date 2327-09-31-1734 6h earlier

"Give me an update" Kane growled angrily into the microphone.

He was standing for this meeting. Normally he would be seated during calls, but this meeting ~~required~~ something
extra. The view of the city bellow, with its concrete jungle sprawl, towering glass monuments, and permanent atmospheric
drear, did nothing to subdue his anger and frustration. He would have to suffice on taking it out on these two.

"We've made progress" Ziatovsicsh chirped nervously.

Kane could not actually see him, as this call was audio only, but he imagined the man begin to sweat and pace about in
whatever room he was in.

"Progress?" Kane echoed, implying further elaboration was required from his head scientist.

"Yes... progress." Ziatovsicsh echoed back in an even more worried tone.

"What he means to say is that we have a plan, and are in the midst of executing it." Knopp cut in, clearly judging that
now was the time to best deploy his political prowess. "We have examined Alphason's patterns and extrapolated his next
destination. I'm at the site now, deploying our troops accordingly. I mean to personally oversee this operation."

Kane liked how the statement made him feel like he had made the correct choice. Knopp had clearly understood his role in
this. _Unlike Ziatovsicsh, who seemed permanently on the back foot._

"Yes, and I helped." Ziatovsicsh added meekly.

Kane had to resist the temptation to physically wince at that statement.

"See doctor! This is what I expect when I ask for updates. Good, actionable updates." Kane yelled into the phone. "I
will be re-considering renewing your contract after this is up. You're proving to be even less capable than your
predecessor." Kane inhaled deeply. "Knopp I want an update as soon as you have him. I want Alphason wiped off the map!
Any means possible! Nobody steals from me and gets away with it, make sure everyone knows that."

"Affirmative sir" Knopp replied gruffly. "I'll update you as soon as I confirm it myself."

Kane felt relief flood his system at the words. The last few hours had been nothing but impossible reports and requests
all stemming from this one problem. He had been bombarded with reports missing persons, stolen data, and malfunctioning
equipment. For some unknown reason everyone believed that he was going to solve their problems. His answer to most of
them had been the same. _Figure it out yourself!_ This one thing had to go right. Then he could pin all the blame,
re-assert himself as the hero, and begin their new power initiative. _Speaking of which._

"Doctor. How about you give me some good news? What of the site? How's what sort of output level projections are we
looking at?"

"Oh, of course! Yes, yes. On that front we've made some important findings." Doctor Ziatovsicsh said, his audible
nervousness slowly fading away with each word. "We've calculated that the growth rate will soon reach exponential. I
expect a ten times to twenty times day over day improvement in output. With those kinds of changes I would like to
revisit our previous conversations about research allocations. I've prepared a report-"

"Ten to twenty?" Kane cut him off.

"Yes. Day over day even." Ziatovsicsh replied earnestly.

"Don't bother sending that report doctor, with that kind of improvement we'll be too focused on expanding our network. I
want you to put all your resources into network optimization. We have to be able to reach as many districts as cheaply
as possible. Put that power to work. I want you to send those projections to accounting and finance. They'll need to
iron out the cost projections for that kind of expansion."

Kane could barely contain his excitement. With that kind of growth he would be able to monopolize several markets.
Uniicorp already had a stranglehold on most regional capitol, but they had run into roadblocks at several regional choke
points. Most of their capitol abroad was financially linked, backed by their monopoly here, but this new development
would allow them to breach those networks and begin exporting the most valuable resource know to humankind where it was
desperately under managed.

"Ziatovsicsh, I want your full focus on this. In fact I want you onsite. You'll report directly to me."

"But sir." Ziatovsicsh stammered. "I think it would be best if I stayed out here, in the field. There's much work to be
done, and I have to be ready to oversee it directly."

"Doctor, I won't repeat myself." Kane growled. "You might not even get to your research projects if you continue
stalling and deflecting. Consider this your final warning."

Kane was at his wits end with this man. Months of poor reports and performance from him, and now that they finally had
some progress he was stalling and asking to work on dead end projects. _He's just been out for too long._ Kane felt that
bringing him back into headquarters would finally break him of these latest issues. He would soon be back to his
obedient and hardworking self.

"Yes, sir." The doctor finally replied, an unmistakable air of defeat in his tone.

"It's done then." Kane said.

He thumbed the icon and ended the call, but not without noticing Knopp had stayed on the line the entire time. _Seems
he's not the block he looks to be._ Kane could not blame the man for being curious. It's not his fault that the doctor
had been overly public with his failings. Call ended, Kane stood there in the tall window, looking out over the dirty
and sullen city. Despite all his work, he still considered it to be one of the biggest failings of his family. Kane felt
that the Carthatchs had miss managed it, and had been too hands-off. He understood that while people longed for peace,
they actually needed hardship. While Kane understood the limits of his corporation and the powers that maintained it, he
had always wondered if it would have been possible to start over. Build from scratch how things should be, rather than
just set up a foundation and hope.

With these new readings, combined with the projections doctor Ziatovsicsh had estimated, Kane knew they could rebuild.
With a near limitless power, he would forever make his mark in the family legacy. His name would be cemented as the key
cornerstone through which all future successes would be derived from.

He stepped back from the window, and walked towards his desk, deciding that a small reward was in order. He sat heavily
in his chair, and thumbed a button on his ancient wooden desk. A thin drawer popped loose, and he reached into it to
retrieve a thin blister pack. He cracked open two onto his desk, and paused. He tapped an icon on his phone, clearing
his throat at the same time.

"Tailings, prep my ride home. We're done for the day." He spoke evenly into the comm.

"Yes sir." The quaint voice responded.

Kane dropped the remaining unopened blisters into the drawer, and firmly slid it shut. He delicately grabbed the two
pills from the desk and popped them both into his mouth at once. Almost at once, he felt a warm feeling creep into him
as they dissolved on his tongue.

"This will be my biggest achievement." Kane said out loud to himself.
