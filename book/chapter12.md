> UNII Corps log date 2327-09-31-2334 5min earlier

Hector found time to be less linear than usual.

It was the strangest sensation. He felt like the current that had been slowing him down, the weighty drag that
constantly restricted his movements, was not actually restricting him, but rather a restriction by himself. He felt, but
had yet been able to verify, that if he strained hard enough, he would be able to pierce the current that encircled him
and see and move through time. Even more confusingly the restriction 'felt' like it was something he was actively doing.
Hector likened it to being in two places at once, with one version fighting to move, the other pushing back to stop
'himself' from moving too fast. His brain struggled to piece it all together.

On top of it all, the others were constantly interrupting him and prodding him forwards. On one hand he was grateful to
be leaving this place, with its terrible tortured victims. The victims. He routinely found himself a silent and
abhorrent witness to the worst crimes imaginable. People disected alive, experimented, disguarded, and them killed.
'Hopefully' killed. With the infinit kaleidoscopic effect Hector possessed, he had witnessed all of it, but been able to
do nothing for it. The scenes had left him scared and hollow, despite the foggy distance between him and reality.

But on the other hand there was something going on. Something close-by that he could feel, more than see, that was
either doing something important or had done something important.

_Time feels so arbitrary._

Before, or maybe now, he had wandered the city, looking for a gap he could follow. He still understood that the only way
forwards was to follow the path in the storm, but he thought that his time with Del, Andrea, Rikki, and then the rest of
them, had come to a close. Somehow they had found him, and their paths seemed to be crossed yet again. In fact, Hector
now felt like they had led him to success. Deep underground, near the tunnels of the old city, Hector could sense
someone else at work. Some indomitable force of time and space had been here, and now he was following in its footsteps.
Hector knew, knew with all his being, that at the end of this journey, he would be made whole again. Returned to his
former self. Woken from this terrible nightmare that he inhabited beyond any definable meaning of time.
