> UNII Corps log date 2327-09-31-1704 8h later

Rikki heard a groan from the cot behind her and turned in her chair. She saw movement and heard rustling that mean only
one thing. _He's finally awake._ She waited until he rotated his head to face her. His eyes were little slits, squinting
even in the dim lighting. Rikki felt her hope fade as she slowly questioned weather he was actually awake, or this was
just another fitful dream. She hung in suspension for what felt like minutes, waiting to see of he would start
convulsing or screaming, or something. He had had a rough time of it. The last eight hours had been fitful, and twice he
had almost thrown himself from the cot. Rikki herself had barely gotten any sleep, but she doubted Del ha gotten any of
substance either. When he, Andrea, and Hector had come stumbling in she had had a minor heart attack. Both Del and
Andrea had collapsed on the spot, and Hector had hardly been any help. He had simply left her to be the pretend medic
and stood against the doorframe staring of into the distance like he did. She had had to drag Del to her cot, lugging
his unconscious dead weight with all her scrawny might. Andrea had had the grace to stay conscious at least and had
willingly accepted Rikki's help in getting across the room, and settling for a crumpled up blanket wedged against the
wall on the cleanest part of Rikki's floor. She had proceeded to fall asleep almost immediately. She had not stayed out
for long though, and she had woken up with an attitude that Rikki did not think she deserved. Without even so much as a
thanks, Andrea had left, complaining about missing check-in or something. _Good riddance, and fuck corpos._ Rikki had
had to bite her tongue earlier, but now she knew it had been a mistake to trust, or even use them. Sitting there, she
waited uncomfortable to see what would come of Del. A moment later, he groaned again, and sat upright holding his head
in one hand and supporting himself with the other.

"Hey Rikki, do you have any water?" His voice rasped.

Relief flooeded through her and she wordlessly got up and strode to her kitchen. It was really just a wall with a bench,
a fridge, and a sink but it's not like she cared to argue with anyone. Come to think of it, the last 10 hours had
probably been the most people she had ever hosted since she had been here. She grabbed a glass from a shelf and filled
it with water. She quietly walked it back to Del, and then hurried to her desk to grab some meds. She fumbled with the
bottles, trying to find the one she was looking for. After a few awkward tries, she finally found the one she was
looking for, and brought it to Del.

"Here, these are good for headaches. Take 2." She said, in her kindest voice.
