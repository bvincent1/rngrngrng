> UNII Corps log date 2327-09-31-1704 8h later

Rikki heard a groan from the cot behind her and turned in her chair. She saw movement and heard rustling that mean only
one thing. _He's finally awake._ She waited until he rotated his head to face her. His eyes were little slits, squinting
even in the dim lighting. Rikki felt her hope fade as she slowly questioned weather he was actually awake, or this was
just another fitful dream. She hung in suspension for what felt like minutes, waiting to see of he would start
convulsing, screaming, or something new. He had had a rough time of it. The last eight hours had been fitful, and twice
he had almost thrown himself from the cot. Rikki herself had barely gotten any sleep, but she doubted Del had gotten any
of substance either. When he, Andrea, and Hector had come stumbling in Rikki had had a minor heart attack. Both Del and
Andrea had collapsed on the spot, and Hector had hardly been any help. He had simply left her to be the pretend medic
and stood against the doorframe staring of into the distance like he did. She had had to drag Del to her cot, lugging
his unconscious dead weight with all her scrawny might.

Andrea had had the politeness to stay conscious at least and had willingly accepted Rikki's help in getting across the
room, and settling for a crumpled up blanket wedged against the wall on the cleanest part of Rikki's floor. She had
proceeded to fall asleep almost immediately. Andrea was still passed out on the floor, curled up and having a much worse
reaction to whatever it was that was affecting them. She had broken out in a cold sweat and shakes, muttering
occasionally like Del. Despite Rikki's hatred for corpos, she had given her water and painkillers. The meds had seemed
to take the edge off and she had drifted off to sleep shortly after. Rikki might hate the corps, but this was just a
person, even if she was a bit bitchy.

Whatever they had gotten up to had clearly done some damage to them. Except Hector. He had stood around, only to leave a
while ago. Something about that guy made her uncomfortable. His whole thing of not looking at anyone and being off in
his own world really got to her.

Sitting there, she waited uncomfortably and anxiously to see what would come of them. Not even just that, but she burned
with curiosity to learn what they had gotten up to. A moment later, Del groaned again, and sat upright holding his head
in one hand and supporting himself with the other.

"Hey Rikki, do you have any water?" His voice rasped.

Relief flooded through her and she wordlessly got up and strode to her kitchen. It was really just a wall with a bench,
a fridge, and a sink but it's not like she anyone cared to argue. The last ten hours had been the most people she had
ever hosted since she had been here, and. She grabbed a glass from a shelf and filled it with water. She quietly walked
it back to Del, and then hurried to her desk to grab some meds. She fumbled with the bottles, trying to find the one she
was looking for. After a few awkward tries, she finally found the one she was looking for, and brought it to Del.

"Here, these are good for headaches. Take 2." She said in her kindest voice.

Del gratefully accepted them and fumbled the lid open. It went spinning and rolling end over end and he awkwardly went
to retrieve it, but she stopped him and went herself. He shook out two pills and swallowed them with a gulp of water.
She handed him the lid, and went to grab her chair intending to sit closer to him.

"What happened to you two? You've both been out for a while, and Hector isn't exactly the talkative type. Not to mention
he left shortly after dropping you both off." She asked.

Del scanned around, clearly confused and trying to get a grip on his bearings.

"Honestly Rikki." He paused. "I don't really know how what happened."

He turned his head to watch Andrea, still curled up on the floor nearby.

"Start at the beginning Del. We need this to work out. You clearly found something, but I haven't heard anything on the
net." Rikki asked insistingly.

"Well we found the place. Wasn't really a church though. Not like any that I've seen anyways. It had a pit, inside, that
lead to some tunnels." Del paused again to sip his water. "We followed them, the tunnels, but there was something, or I
guess someone watching us." He looked up at her, a spark of recognition in his eyes.

"They were waiting for someone else! Alphason! Or Hector." He radiated energy now, as is the very mention of the name
had invigorated him.

Rikki tried to settle him, clumsily motioning him to elaborate. "Wait wait wait, who was waiting for you? Or the other
guy, or whatever, but who was it? Some church members did this to you?" Rikki asked, almost incredulously.

"No, no. I mean yes. But like not normal church people. Or normal people for that matter." Del's face narrowed, clearly
deep in thought. "They had been changed somehow. Mutated, or something." Then in a much quieter voice, "Evolved."

"So you escaped them? But how does Alphason and Hector fit into this?" He asked, pulling away to her desk, intending to
start researching this new name.

"Well not quite." Del coughed slightly then continued. "They kinda captured me, but Andrea, and Hector, rescued me and
we escaped together out through a loading elevator."

Rikki stopped part ways through typing to look at Del, then Andrea. She had a tough time believing that this woman had
done anything so selfless as to help him without some sort of motive.

"You're sure that's what happened?" Rikki asked incredulously. "I kinda doubt this bitch would do anything but save her
own skin."

Del looked back at her with a look of confusion. "I'm just telling you what happened. Why don't we just ask her? Or
Hector. Do you know where he went?"

"No. It's not like he even offered before he up and left. Besides didn't you say these church guys were looking to find
him, or meet or something?" Rikki turned back to her screens, and resumed her research. She still wanted to learn more
about this 'Alphason', and see if they could somehow link him to this puzzle.

"Well, that's what this one guy said. He mentioned an apostle. Kinda made me think of him. Of course he could have been
speaking metaphorically. Besides that's not the dangerous part. It's a cult."

Rikki could tell Del had paused, trying to emphasize for dramatic effect.

"Yeah, most religions are." She brushed him off, not even bothering to break away from her new work.

"No, it's literally a cult. Like with literal brainwashing. They have some sort of tech that they attach you too and
then they zap your mind. That's what they did to me."

"So wait, they hooked you up to this machine, and then what? What did you see?" She asked, curious, and concerned, but
she was dedicated to finishing this task first.

"Nothing." Del said. "I didn't see anything, the next thing I knew Andrea had blown the bucket clean off my head and
then I was fighting for my life against a bunch of them. Oh shit I forgot about my neck!" Del yelled.

Rikki could not stop herself from getting distracted this time, and turned to see Del had pulled his jacket off his neck
to reveal a blood caked wound of tiny holes in a semi circle just next to his neck.

"Are you fucking kidding me?!" She screamed at him. "Did you just do that?! Did that cult do that?! Did you just bleed
all over my cot?! Jesus fucking christ!"

And she took off from her work to get the remainder of her medical supplies. At this rate she was going to need to
restock in a few hours. _For all I know that corpo is bleeding out into my bedroll too. Would serve her right though._
She hurried for her kit. She hauled the entire box to the cot, and kneeling started sorting through the contents. Del
had gently peeled the caked on jacket from his neck and tiny pricks of fresh blood welled up from the freshly disturbed
scabs. He seemed nonplussed by the injury, clearly having forgotten about it before he had sat upright. Rikki finally
fished out the supplies and gently set them down on the cot next to him.

"Sorry Rikki. I kinda forgot to check it." He said, clearly embarrassed.

"You're only apologizing because you need my help this time. And you want your clothes in one piece." She said, gently
peeling back the rest of his shirt to check for anything else along his back. She saw that the wound was actually a
rough circle with tiny but deep jabs in roughly even spacing.

"What did this? Was it that brainwashing bucket you mentioned?" She asked while simultaneously manipulating Del back and
forth to catch the light better.

"No it was a human. One of the cult members. But they were different. I didn't really get a good look at em." Del
paused. "I can stand, lets move over to the table."

Rikki stepped back, and watched closely as Del stood. He was remarkably sturdy having just been through what could only
be described as a descent into hell. Del leaned over, and Rikki half jumped to catch him, but stopped when she realized
he was just reaching for the supplies she had unloaded. With the awkward bundle in his arms he strode to the table, lay
them out in a pile, and sat at the only chair, his front to the chair-back. She followed him, and watched as he
carefully pulled off him jacket, then his pulled his shirt over his head. Rikki did her best to start the conversation
again, trying to distract herself and him.

"So what some cultist just jumped up and bit you, then they strapped a bucket to your head? And left these marks in your
shoulder? I've heard of wild body mods, but not like this. Monster teeth? Implants sure, but teeth?" She asked
incredulously.

"I dunno." He replied then gasped quietly as she applied the anti-septic. "I don't think it was a body mod. Something
about these guys. Makes me think it wasn't something that happened before they got taken." He trailed off in thought.

"So they're abducting people, brainwashing them, then modding them and for what? Some religious army? I mean I guess."
Rikki talked quickly and loudly. She was nervous, though for the life of her she could not decide why. Maybe it was all
these people. Maybe she just needed sleep.
