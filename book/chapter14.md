> UNII Corps log date 2327-10-01-0004 6.5h later

Del felt the weight with each step. In the cold, dark, and damp tunnels it felt as is the very concrete above them
weighed him down. They had not been walking for long, but after all that had happened, he was unsure how much longer he
could keep going. If he strained, Del felt like he could hear pounding coming from behind them. Deep in the tunnel, he
convinced himself of a rhythmic thumping of cold metal on metal. It hastened his steps, and kept him moving despite the
pain that was welling up inside him.

They trudged onwards, through puddles and darkness. He and the others, all worse for wear, but unable to stop. He could
hear an occasional breath and the patter of their boots in shallow water, but otherwise they were all minding their own.
Everyone was keeping pace. Nobody wanted to give hint of weakness or ask to wait. Nobody wanted to stop out of fear.
Fear of getting caught, fear of what lay behind them, or fear of what lay ahead of them. Their meager lights only
illuminated the barest of trails in the dark. Multiple times during their short escape Del had almost tripped on
upheaved steel track or piles of broken concrete. But together, they kept jogging.

He was almost completely out of breath when they came upon a bend with faint light falling just beyond. O'Rhonn signaled
them all to halt, and Del gingerly set the dead weight down. Andrea watched him closely, clearly worried that Hector was
still unconscious. Del knelt down next to him out of exhaustion, and struggled to summon the willpower to do much beyond
ready himself. _He'll have to wait._ His hands fumbled with the rifle strapped to his chest, and he desperately tried to
will them into functioning properly. A firm hand clasped his shoulder, and Del looked up to see Tokolov with a
comforting look on his face.

Del had not had much of a chance to express his feelings before, having been expressly wrapped up in the storm of events
that followed, but he felt a sorrow well up deep inside him. He had worked hard to try and bury this sorrow, but having
them back, even if only a few, brought back emotions that Del had no time for here and now. What made it even harder for
Del was seeing the jagged scar that snaked out from under the patch Tokolov wore over his one eye. The injury was new to
Del, but clearly not to Tokolov. The wound was silvery and extended out from cheek to temple, peeking out of both sides
from underneath the makeshift cloth that made up the patch covering his face. The old man's hair was still dark, but Del
thought there was more silver in it than he remembered, bringing Del to wonder if his time since they had last been a
team had been harsheer than his own. His gaze must have lingered a bit too long, as Tokolov screwed up his face in an
awkward sort of embarrassment. The hand released him, and Tokolov strode to the front of their little formation.

Del found he could properly ready his weapon now and he did so as quickly as he could. There was faint mutering coming
from O'Rhonn and the rest, and Del finally stood and made to join them.

"Munrhoe, Tokolov, you two with me. We're gonna check out the light. Sigs, wait here with O'Delt and our guests."
O'Rhonn whispered.

"Wait, I'm good. I can do it." Del gasped. He still out of breath, but his energy was coming back to him.

"It's not up for debate." O'Rhonn whispered harshly at him.

"Hey, give the kid a break. He's still one of us." Tokolov spoke quietly, but with genuine praise on his tone.

"I'm not doing this right now." O'Rhonn said, clearly running out of patience. "My orders stand. Let's go." Without
another word, he spun and slunk away into the darkness.

Del watched as the other two joined O'Rhonn, their forms melting into the shadows on either side of the tunnel. Del felt
his shoulders dip and a sudden wave of exhaustion washed over him.

"What was that about?" He quietly asked, turning to Sigmund.

"Dunno, but let's get you checked over." Sigmund "And your friends." He hastily added.

"I'm good, lets check with her and then him."

Sigmond tapped Del on the arm, right where a hole revealed a filthy patch of bandage underneath.

"Oh, that's from earlier. Let's get them first."

Del turned to see that Andrea was milling around with her gun slung over her shoulder. She was clearly not in hurry one
way or the other. Del felt a gentle prod at his shoulder and found that Sigmund was trying to gesture something to him.
Del had to concentrate to bring it back, but after a moment he recognized their shared language and understood what
Sigmund was trying to ask.

"Hey Andrea, this is Sigmund. He's gonna look you over, make sure you're not too banged up." Del said.

Andrea's head snapped to face them, and Del immediately emotionally braced himself for a stream of angry words to sweep
him away. To his surprise, she merely shrugged. Sigmund took that as acceptance, and went to examine her. Del decided to
follow him, curious to ask her something.

"So what was all that earlier?" Del asked.

"You'll have to be more specific." Andrea said, exasperation in her voice.

"In the lab. You were off." Del clarified.

Del saw Sigmund tap a finger on her shoulder, and she turned her head to face him. He raised his hands defensively.
"Your plate took a hit. Need to double-check." Sigmund said hastily.

"Oh."
